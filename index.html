About

I'm a Business Analyst with 5+ years of professional experience in business operations, administration, project coordination and stakeholder engagement. I bring a structured, analytical approach to understanding business needs, identifying process gaps and turning complex challenges into practical, business-focused solutions.

Through hands-on business analysis projects and professional training, I've developed expertise in requirements analysis, business process mapping, gap analysis and data visualisation, using Microsoft Excel and Power BI to generate insights and support evidence-based decisions.

I'm passionate about bridging the gap between business requirements and effective solutions that optimise processes, improve operational efficiency and deliver measurable business value.

How I work

  1. Understand the problem, the stakeholders and what success looks like.
  2. Analyse processes and data to find gaps and root causes.
  3. Recommend prioritised, practical solutions.
  4. Communicate findings through clear documents and dashboards.
